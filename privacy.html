Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 25, 2026

The short version

Planner is a desktop app that runs on your own computer. It has no server. Everything it gets from the platforms you connect stays on your computer, and nothing is sold or shared with anyone.

What Planner reads and why

Only after you connect an account and approve the permissions, and only for that account:

  • TikTok profile (user.info.basic): your display name and avatar, to show which account is connected.
  • TikTok posting (video.upload, video.publish): to send your videos to your TikTok inbox or post them, with the settings you choose for each post. Before a direct post, Planner reads your account's posting options (such as who can watch) to show them to you.
  • TikTok videos (video.list): your own public videos and their view, like, comment and share counts, shown in Planner's Socials tab.
  • YouTube and Facebook: the same kind of data for those accounts (channel or Page name, your videos, their statistics, comments and Page messages), to publish and to show statistics, comments and messages so you can reply.

Where it is kept

On your computer, in Planner's data folder. Sign-in tokens are encrypted with Windows' own protection. Planner sends data only to the platform it belongs to (for example, a video you post to TikTok goes to TikTok's servers) and to no one else. There are no analytics, ads or trackers.

Keeping and deleting it

Disconnecting an account in Planner deletes its tokens. Deleting Planner's data folder (%APPDATA%\Planner) deletes everything. You can also remove Planner's access at any time from the platform's own settings (on TikTok: Settings and privacy → Security → Apps and services permissions).

Children

Planner is not meant for children under 13 (or the minimum age for the platform in your country).
