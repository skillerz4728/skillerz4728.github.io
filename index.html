Planner

A desktop app for Windows that schedules and publishes the short explainer videos of the How Things Work channel to its own YouTube, TikTok and Facebook accounts.

What it does

  • Puts videos on a calendar and publishes them at the chosen time, from the owner's PC.
  • On TikTok, sends a video to the account's TikTok inbox to finish in the app, or posts it directly with the settings the owner picks for each post (who can watch, comments, duets, stitches).
  • Shows the account's own videos with their views, likes, comments and shares, so the owner can see which videos do best.

Where your data lives

Planner runs on the owner's computer. Videos, posts, statistics and sign-in tokens are stored on that computer only, encrypted where they are logins. Planner has no server and no user accounts of its own.
